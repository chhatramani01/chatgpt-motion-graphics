# 55 · ISOTYPE Pictograms

**Category:** Data & Information

## Visual direction
Vienna Method pictorial statistics: rows of identical flat pictograms where each symbol is a fixed quantity, partial symbols for remainders, three muted litho inks on a strict grid with geometric sans lettering.

## Animation grammar
Title and key set first, the year column drops in, then symbols are stamped out row by row from the centre in stepped, rubber-stamp timing, the 2025 row of machines racing ahead; the litho paper breathes in the hold.

## Explainer structure
A Vienna-Method picture-statistic told across one long chart sheet the camera pans along: symbols are stamped in stepped 12 fps timing, flat Isotype arrows show flow, numerals are stamped and re-stamped, and accuracy is counted in rows of tick symbols with partial symbols for fractions.
