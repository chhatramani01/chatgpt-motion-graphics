# 07 · Papercraft Models

**Category:** Popular Explainer Looks

## Visual direction
Printed-cardstock kit models: boxy low-poly robots, cats and charts assembled from flat printed faces with dotted fold lines, glue tabs and hairline seam gaps, Lambert-lit by a warm key light on a green self-healing cutting mat with craft tools at the edges.

## Animation grammar
Flat printed templates fold themselves up into 3D models on twos with a stop-motion jitter, pieces hop into place and tags fold into tents, while the camera slides smoothly across the mat.

## Explainer structure
One long cutting mat: the camera slides from beat to beat while flat printed templates fold themselves up into the robot, cards, bars and tent labels (on twos); arrows are creased paper strips that unroll along the mat, labels are tent tags dropped onto what they name, counters are printed tent cards re-posed each step, and the wrong guess gets a camera push-in and a red flag that pops and later spins to green.
