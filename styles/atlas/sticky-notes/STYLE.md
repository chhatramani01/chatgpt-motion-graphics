# 64 · Sticky-Note Wall

**Category:** Illustration

## Visual direction
A brainstorming cork board: curling Post-its with adhesive bands and lifted-corner shadows, marker handwriting and doodles, a taped index card, push pins and red yarn connecting the ideas.

## Animation grammar
Notes slap onto the board one by one with a tiny bounce, then red yarn unspools from pin to pin linking the ideas.

## Explainer structure
A detective-board camera move across one big cork wall: notes slap in on cue, marker arrows and red yarn draw the flow, numbers are crossed out and rewritten by hand, and red marker circles the key moments.
