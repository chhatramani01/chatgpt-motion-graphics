# 20 · Kawaii Anime

**Category:** Popular Explainer Looks

## Visual direction
Flat 2D kawaii anime illustration: chibi characters with big glossy anime eyes and blush marks, chunky rounded shapes in strawberry-milk pink, mint, butter, lavender and sky, soft plum outlines of even weight, one cel-shadow tone and a small highlight, on polka-dot and star patterns.

## Animation grammar
Everything pops in on twos with bouncy squash-and-stretch, characters bob and wiggle, sparkles twinkle, and scenes change through star and heart iris wipes.

## Explainer structure
A chibi robot mascot acts the lesson out with anime reaction grammar (swirl eyes, sweat drop and "!?" on the wrong guess, star eyes and confetti at 97%); scenes change through star and heart iris wipes, connectors are dotted candy arrows, and the counters are bouncing outlined numerals.
