# 12 · Bioluminescent Deep Sea

**Category:** Light & Imaging

## Visual direction
The midnight zone: near-black water with parallax marine snow, and nothing lit but living light — a jellyfish whose nerve net glows like a neural network, a siphonophore chain, a rainbow-rippling comb jelly and an anglerfish lure. Additive cyan, teal and electric-blue glow with magenta and amber accents; type is bioluminescent.

## Animation grammar
The camera sinks (marine snow streams upward) as animals switch on one by one: bell rims pulse, nerve-net nodes flicker alight, the siphonophore lights segment by segment, the lure blinks on, and the words form out of gathering plankton.

## Explainer structure
A descent through the midnight zone: the question forms from gathering plankton, cat and dog swarms ride a glowing current into a jellyfish model whose nerve net is its weights, the error ripples back through the net in magenta, accuracy climbs a siphonophore chain to a plankton bloom-burst, and transitions are a descent, a sweeping plankton bloom and an anglerfish lure lighting new water.
