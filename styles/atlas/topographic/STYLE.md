# 70 · Topographic Survey

**Category:** Data & Information

## Visual direction
A vintage survey quadrangle of a "loss landscape": brown contour lines with heavier labelled index contours, soft hillshade and woodland tint, a water-lined lake, a red dashed switchback trail and a full printed collar with scale bar and north arrow.

## Animation grammar
Printed plate by plate: the black frame and collar, then the grey hillshade, the green woodland sliding into register, the blue lake, then brown contours rising from the shore like a tide; streams trace downhill, names land, and the red trail walks itself down the switchbacks while the camera eases in.

## Explainer structure
The loss landscape is literally the map: survey points (examples) raise the terrain, a red hiker guesses, checks the slope and steps down a dashed switchback trail while the camera pans along it, and accuracy is lettered in a map cartouche like an elevation figure.
