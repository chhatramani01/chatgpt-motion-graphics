# 53 · Vaporwave

**Category:** Retro UI & Neon

## Visual direction
Pastel-neon dreamscape: sliced sunset sun, glowing grid floor, a glitching marble bust on a checkerboard plinth, palm silhouettes, katakana, and a chewed-up VHS tape finish.

## Animation grammar
Tape starts on a blue VCR screen and rolls into lock, the striped sun rises, palms slide in, the bust glitches into existence slice by slice, katakana stamps on with chroma split and a tracking band crawls down.

## Explainer structure
Told on a worn VHS tape: scenes change with a tape cut (VCR blue screen, fast-forward, vertical roll), the header lives on as the VCR on-screen menu, the counter is tape-counter digits, labelled polaroids slide along the neon floor into the marble-bust "model", a pastel guess.exe pop-up does the guessing and the chart is drawn in neon across the sky.
