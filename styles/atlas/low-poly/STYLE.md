# 40 · Low-Poly Faceted

**Category:** Dimensional

## Visual direction
Faceted low-poly 3D: a triangulated noise terrain with flat per-face Lambert shading, atmospheric depth fog, cone trees and a faceted rocket lifting off into a dawn gradient sky.

## Animation grammar
Terrain facets flip into place from the camera out to the horizon, trees and the launch pad pop up, then the rocket ignites and lifts off on a faceted flame and smoke column while the camera dollies forward.

## Explainer structure
A continuous camera flight through one faceted valley (overview → river of labelled example cards → floating crystal model whose facets realign → pillar chart on a plateau → pull back), with angular two-tone ribbon arrows, cut-corner tags pinned to objects and Space Grotesk counters.
