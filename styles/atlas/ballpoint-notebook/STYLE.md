# 68 · Ballpoint Notebook

**Category:** Hand-Drawn

## Visual direction
A blue Bic doodle in the back of a college-ruled notebook: dense crosshatching for darks, pressure-varied lines with ink blobs where strokes start, bubble lettering with a hatched drop shadow and compulsive margin doodles.

## Animation grammar
Lines scratch on stroke by stroke, then layers of crosshatching build up the shadows pass by pass, while the margin doodles get traced over and over.

## Explainer structure
Doodled down one long college-ruled page: the pen draws while the camera scrolls along and then down the page, headings are bubble letters with crosshatched shadows, boxes get hatched drop-shadows, a red pen marks the wrong guess, and counters are re-inked in bubble numerals.
