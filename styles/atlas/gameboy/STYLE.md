# 59 · Pocket Monochrome

**Category:** Retro Digital

## Visual direction
An original DMG handheld frame: 160x144 pixels in exactly four pea-soup greens, a visible LCD pixel grid with each pixel casting a faint shadow on the reflective backing, motion ghosting, and the purple-grey bezel with its striped DOT MATRIX label.

## Animation grammar
A handheld boot: the logo scrolls down one LCD pixel per frame and smears, the palette steps to white, the overworld steps back in through the 4 shades, the hero walks on 1 px per frame with a 2-frame cycle, and the window pops up and prints text.

## Explainer structure
Explains as a handheld monster-battle RPG in four LCD shades: the question text box slides up the window layer into a header, palette fades act as door transitions, labelled cards march an arrow-tile path into CHIP the model, a flash-and-stripes battle intro starts a GUESS/CHECK/ADJUST fight (hit flash, dotted error trail, weight bars), and a LEVEL UP stats screen counts accuracy to an inverted 97%.
