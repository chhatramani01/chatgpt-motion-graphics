# 33 · Etch A Sketch Toy

**Category:** Hand-Drawn

## Visual direction
The classic red line-drawing toy: glossy rounded red plastic with hard specular streaks, two white knurled knobs, and a grainy silver aluminium-powder screen where everything is one thin dark line that never lifts: crisp horizontals and verticals, stair-stepped diagonals and curves, and visible travel lines joining every letter and shape.

## Animation grammar
A shake clears the last doodle in patchy streaks, then the single line crawls out at time-lapse speed while the left knob turns with every horizontal move and the right knob with every vertical one.

## Explainer structure
Every word and picture is one continuous stylus line with the knobs turning to match; the scene transition is shaking the toy so the drawing fades in streaks (leaving a faint ghost), arrows and labels are part of the same line (labels ride arrows and bubble edges), and numbers grow as they are written: 1,000,000 gains a zero at a time and the accuracy staircase writes 61%, 70%, 77%, 90% on its treads before a double-traced, boxed 97%.
