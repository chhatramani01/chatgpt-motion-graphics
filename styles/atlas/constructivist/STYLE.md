# 58 · Constructivist Propaganda

**Category:** Art & Design Movements

## Visual direction
A 1920s Rodchenko / El Lissitzky agitprop poster: red, black and cream only, a halftone photomontage robot bellowing through a megaphone, beams radiating from its voice, a red disc and hard diagonal bars, and heavy condensed lettering set on the slant — litho ink mottle on cheap paper.

## Animation grammar
Agitprop staccato: the red disc rolls in, the halftone robot slides up the diagonal, beams punch out of the megaphone two frames at a time, the slogan bars shoot across and the words slam down on the beat; in the hold the beam fan pulses with each shout.

## Explainer structure
Agitprop sequence: Lissitzky's red wedge drives across the sheet between scenes, slogans and labels slam onto slanted bars on twos, arrows are heavy bars with wedge heads, the counter is stencil-tall Anton, and the key moments get a stamped red cross or a burst of beams.
