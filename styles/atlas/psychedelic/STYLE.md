# 05 · Psychedelic Poster

**Category:** Cosmic & Psychedelic

## Visual direction
1960s San Francisco concert-poster psychedelia: full-strength inks in vibrating complementary pairs, liquid churning ring bands, sunbursts and a mirror-symmetric eye-flower, with bulbous melting Shrikhand lettering on worn poster stock.

## Animation grammar
Colour ripples out from the centre, petals unfurl and the eye opens, then the lettering melts into place while the liquid bands keep slowly churning.

## Explainer structure
The model is a giant eye-flower whose petals are its weights: examples ride striped ribbons into it, the wrong guess makes the inks clash and jitter, the correction folds the swirl into a symmetric mandala, 97% bursts into a kaleidoscope sunburst; scenes change by kaleidoscope rotation, a liquid melt and an iris ripple.
