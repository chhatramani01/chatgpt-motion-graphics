# 71 · Pointillist Afternoon

**Category:** Painterly

## Visual direction
Neo-Impressionist divisionism: every form is built from small dabs of unmixed pigment — complementary hues side by side that blend optically — with the canvas weave peeking between the dots and a dotted painted border.

## Animation grammar
Dots shimmer as individual dabs re-pick their hue each frame while the overall colour holds, like light vibrating on the water; the sailboat drifts and the parasol sways.

## Explainer structure
Every frame re-dabs one fixed field of pure-pigment dots against the current scene, so moving things shimmer as dots re-pick their hue; scene changes are the canvas being re-dabbed in a diagonal sweep, and lettering, arrows, counters and the emphasis rings are all fine dabs.
