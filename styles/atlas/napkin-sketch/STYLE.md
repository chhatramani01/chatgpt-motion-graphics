# 50 · Napkin Sketch

**Category:** Hand-Drawn

## Visual direction
A back-of-the-napkin startup pitch in black Sharpie: ink feathering into soft tissue fibers, an embossed napkin border, quarter-fold creases and crumples, a coffee ring, all lying on a warm wooden cafe table.

## Animation grammar
The marker scrawls each box and arrow in quick confident strokes while ink slowly feathers outward; the napkin breathes slightly as if just set down.

## Explainer structure
A cafe-table pitch: the camera starts tight on the scrawled question then pulls back to the napkin; when a napkin is full a fresh one is slapped down on top of the pile (with a red Sharpie for the wrong guess and the 97%), arrows and numbers are fast Sharpie scrawls that feather into the tissue.
