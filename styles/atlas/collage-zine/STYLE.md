# 35 · Punk Zine Collage

**Category:** Paper & Craft

## Visual direction
A photocopied cut-and-paste zine spread: torn halftone scraps, ransom-note letters cut from mismatched magazines, masking tape and staples, crushed to high-contrast black toner with one fluoro-pink spot ink overprinted off-register.

## Animation grammar
Scraps slam onto the page one per beat with a copier-flash flicker, ransom letters jitter in stop-motion, toner noise boils every frame.

## Explainer structure
Shot on 8 fps steps, each beat is a new photocopied sheet slapped down over the last (copier flash on impact); arrows are marker scrawls, labels are typed strips and ransom letters pasted onto the thing they name, the counter is re-cut from magazine letters every time it ticks, and the wrong guess gets slashed with the pink spot ink.
