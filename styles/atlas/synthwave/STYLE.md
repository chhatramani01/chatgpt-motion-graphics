# 27 · Synthwave Outrun

**Category:** Retro UI & Neon

## Visual direction
80s outrun poster: neon grid highway to a striped sunset, wireframe vector mountains, chrome-beveled italic title with a horizon reflection and a hot-pink neon script, all bathed in bloom.

## Animation grammar
Stars blink on, the horizon line and grid race toward camera, the striped sun rises and wireframe mountains grow, the chrome title slams in with a flash, the script tube flickers on and glints sweep the chrome.

## Explainer structure
A night drive explains it: labelled example cards ride the neon highway into a wireframe AI core, a dashboard HUD holds a rolling odometer counter, synth-knob weights and GUESS/CHECK/ADJUST neon tubes; the camera tilts up into the sky for a glowing accuracy chart and back down for a chrome payoff.
