# 49 · Gouache Travel Poster

**Category:** Painterly

## Visual direction
Mid-century travel poster hand-painted in gouache: six flat, opaque, matte colours stacked in receding layers, dry-brush streaks where the paint thins at each edge, and a bold hand-lettered block.

## Animation grammar
Layers slide in parallax like stacked paper cut-outs, the sun rises behind the ridges, steam puffs bob from the cooling towers and server windows blink on in rows.

## Explainer structure
A run of hand-painted posters: each scene change is a fresh coat of cream swept across the board before the next poster is painted flat colour by flat colour; arrows are flat poster arrow shapes, numbers are repainted in the lettering band, and an orange starburst stamps the key moments.
