# 11 · Editorial Essay Collage

**Category:** Popular Explainer Looks

## Visual direction
The editorial "animated essay" collage: warm grainy paper, archival halftone prints with torn white borders and soft shadows, newspaper clippings in serif type with a translucent yellow highlighter sweep, masking tape, flat colour blocks and big cut-out condensed headlines.

## Animation grammar
Cutouts drop and slide in on 12 fps hand-animated steps while the camera pushes smoothly through parallax layers; the highlighter sweeps left to right and a red marker rings the key detail.

## Explainer structure
Scenes are joined by fast blurred camera whips that hide the cut, plus a new paper sheet slid up on steps; arrows are thin ink route lines and red marker, numbers are re-cut condensed numerals on paper strips, and the emphasis is the yellow highlighter sweep (plus a red marker ring and cross on the wrong guess).
