# 75 · Bitmap Paint Doodle

**Category:** Retro UI & Neon

## Visual direction
A proudly crude early-2000s bitmap painting: aliased pencil lines, real flood fills that leak through gaps, airbrush speckle, double-drawn rectangles and an opaque text-tool box, all inside a grey paint-program window.

## Animation grammar
Strokes appear as if dragged by a mouse in real time, the bucket floods regions in one instant pop (and leaks), the airbrush speckles accumulate while held, and the text cursor blinks.

## Explainer structure
Someone paints the explanation live on one wide bitmap: the camera move is the canvas scrolling (scrollbar thumb sliding), arrows are line/curve-tool strokes, examples pile up via Ctrl+V pastes, numbers are retyped in a text box, and emphasis is a fat red brush X and an ellipse-tool circle.
