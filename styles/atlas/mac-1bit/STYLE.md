# 85 · MacPaint 1984

**Category:** Retro Digital

## Visual direction
Pure 1-bit black & white at the original 72-dpi grain: real Atkinson-dithered grayscale (blown-out highlights, crunchy shadows), 8x8 fill patterns, striped title bars, marching ants and bitmap type.

## Animation grammar
Built the way a 1984 user would: the dithered image scans in row by row, then pencil, line, bucket, rectangle and text tools each switch on in the palette and lay down their marks in snappy, unanimated 1-bit steps, ending on crawling marching ants.

## Explainer structure
Explains with the 1984 desktop itself: an alert asks the question and, once OK is clicked, zoom-rects turn it into the window title; labelled Finder icons are dragged into a Model icon while the item count climbs; a Model window cycles Guess/Check/Adjust buttons with a stop alert and menu-bar beep flash, dashed error arrow and Control-Panel weight sliders; a pattern-filled chart climbs to 97% inside marching ants.
