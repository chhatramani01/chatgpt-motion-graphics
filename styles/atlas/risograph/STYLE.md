# 17 · Risograph Zine

**Category:** Print

## Visual direction
Three fluorescent spot inks (pink, medium blue, yellow) laid down as separate drum passes and multiplied on off-white stock — misregistered plates, grainy patchy solids, dotted tints and roller streaks.

## Animation grammar
The sheet goes through the drum three times — yellow, then pink, then blue roll down the page and slide into misregistration — then the print boils at 8 fps while the rocket creeps upward and its flame flickers in the screen.

## Explainer structure
Scenes change by re-registering the plates — yellow, pink and blue slide off and the next scene's plates slide in one drum pass at a time — with dotted blue flow arrows, pink error arrows and scribbled circles, and counters set in big two-plate zine type.
