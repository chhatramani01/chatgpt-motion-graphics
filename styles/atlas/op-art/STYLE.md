# 69 · Op Art Sphere

**Category:** Generative & Type

## Visual direction
Hard-edged black/ivory checkerboard that swells into a lens-like sphere and ripples at the margins — Vasarely and Riley optical vibration, flat acrylic on primed canvas.

## Animation grammar
A flat checkerboard first ripples into Riley folds, then swells into a lens-sphere that pops past its size and settles; red squares flip in cell by cell from the centre, then the field breathes and the folds drift.

## Explainer structure
The model is the Vasarely lens: untrained its grid is melted noise, the error travels back as an optical ripple through the checkerboard and each adjustment combs the sphere toward perfect order; scenes change with a Riley fold that crushes the picture into crowded columns, labels are hard-edged black and red slabs, and the numbers are Archivo Black on ivory bands.
