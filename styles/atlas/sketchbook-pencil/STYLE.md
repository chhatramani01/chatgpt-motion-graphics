# 78 · Sketchbook Graphite

**Category:** Hand-Drawn

## Visual direction
A graphite study in a spiral sketchbook: faint construction circles and axes under confident darker contours, directional hatching that breaks on the paper tooth, smudged tones, kneaded-eraser highlights and a margin note.

## Animation grammar
Construction lines ghost in first, contours firm up over them, then hatching sweeps across the shadow side while the eraser lifts the highlight.

## Explainer structure
A pencil works across a wide sketchbook spread (faint construction pass first, then a firm contour, crescent hatching for form) while the camera follows, then the page physically turns to a fresh sheet for the progress chart; numbers are pencilled and re-pencilled as they count.
