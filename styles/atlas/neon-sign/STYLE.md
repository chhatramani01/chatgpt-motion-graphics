# 19 · Neon Sign at Night

**Category:** Light & Imaging

## Visual direction
Hand-bent glass neon on an old brick wall after rain: white-hot tube cores, saturated colour halos, painted-out blockout tube, clips and electrodes, light spilling across the brickwork and smearing in the wet pavement.

## Animation grammar
Tubes buzz on letter by letter with a stutter, one failing letter keeps flickering, and the puddle reflection shivers as drops land.

## Explainer structure
One long brick wall the camera whip-pans along: every beat is a new neon sign that buzzes on tube by tube, arrows are segmented chase-light tubes that ignite toward their target, numbers are neon digits re-lit as they change, and the emphasis is a blinking red tube with a ring.
