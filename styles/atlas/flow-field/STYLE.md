# 84 · Flow Field

**Category:** Data & Information

## Visual direction
Thousands of hairline streamlines traced through a curl-noise field that parts around an unseen core; additive colour from a refined gradient builds luminous braids on dark paper.

## Animation grammar
The dark core appears first, then particles are released in a wave from left to right and trace their streamlines outward until the braids glow; in the hold the field slowly breathes while bright sparks ride the currents.

## Explainer structure
The model is the dark core of a flow field: labelled particles spiral into it as data, the camera pushes in as a new particle is carried out the wrong exit ("dog?"), a coral counter-current carries the error back, the circulation re-combs the whole field so the next one exits "cat", and training is shown as the turbulent field combing into smooth order, all captioned in hairline Space Mono.
