# 32 · Sumi-e Ink Wash

**Category:** Painterly

## Visual direction
Monochrome Japanese ink painting on fibrous rice paper: dilute grey washes that bleed and fade into mist, a few confident loaded-brush strokes that split into dry bristle tracks, vast negative space, a vertical calligraphy column and a vermilion seal.

## Animation grammar
Mist drifts sideways across the peaks while each brush stroke is laid down in one sweep, bristles running dry as it travels; the tiny device on the ledge pulses with light.

## Explainer structure
Told along a handscroll the camera unrolls, with mist drifting across between scenes; arrows and the chart are single loaded brush strokes that run dry, labels and numbers are ink-written in place, and vermilion strokes and seal stamps do the emphasis.
