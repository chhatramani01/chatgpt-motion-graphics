# 13 · Product Launch Reveal

**Category:** Popular Explainer Looks

## Visual direction
A big-tech keynote product film: one glossy hero object on a near-black stage, lit like a studio shot with soft key, rim light, a sweeping specular highlight and a floor reflection, huge tight-tracked sans headlines, gradient key numbers with a soft glow and hairline spec callouts.

## Animation grammar
Rim light finds the object in the dark, the key light fades up as the camera slowly orbits and pushes in, a highlight glides across the glass, the edge ring ignites, then the headline arrives one word at a time and hairline callouts draw out to their spec labels.

## Explainer structure
Told as a keynote launch film around one hero object (the model, a glass-topped chip): one continuous studio camera that orbits and pushes between beats, hairline callouts and arrows for flow, a clean red strike for the wrong guess while the chip’s inner layers light up backwards to adjust, a giant violet-to-orange 97% with a glow bloom and gleam, and a light-sweep wipe to a pristine white stage for the tagline.
