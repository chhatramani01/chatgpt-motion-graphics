# 47 · Tarot Card

**Category:** Illustration

## Visual direction
An aged art-nouveau tarot trump on crushed velvet: bold even outlines, flat muted lithograph colours, gold-foil halos and borders, celestial symbols and a worn, foxed cardstock with frayed corners.

## Animation grammar
The card is dealt and flips onto the velvet; a sheen of light slides across the gold foil while the orbiting planets drift around the automaton.

## Explainer structure
Told as a tarot reading: cards are dealt, flipped and gathered off the velvet as the scene changes, gold filigree arrows link the spread, brass plaques count the examples and the accuracy, and a burst of gold light marks the wrong guess and the 97%.
