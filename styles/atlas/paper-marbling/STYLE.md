# 54 · Ebru Paper Marbling

**Category:** Generative & Type

## Visual direction
Turkish ebru on laid paper: a battal stone ground of floated pigments raked into gel-git chevrons and combed, then suminagashi ring-drops pulled by a stylus into tulips — built with the exact drop and tine transforms.

## Animation grammar
Colour drops rain onto the size and bloom, shoving earlier drops into veins; the rake drags the stone into gel-git flames; ring after ring is dropped at one point; then the stylus pulls the rings into a tulip, and a wet sheen glides over the finished bath.

## Explainer structure
The bath is the model: examples land as colour drops, a new picture is a red probe drop that the flow drags toward its guess, the error is a stylus counter-stroke and the adjustment a comb pass that puts the pattern in order; scenes change by lifting the printed sheet off the bath, labels are bookbinder paper labels and arrows are inked stylus strokes.
