# 60 · Botanical Plate

**Category:** Illustration

## Visual direction
A hand-coloured copperplate natural-history plate: swelling engraved hatching that follows every form, loose transparent watercolour tints laid over the print, numbered figures, Latin italic captions and foxed rag paper with a pressed plate mark.

## Animation grammar
The circuit-flower sways on its stem while the copper beetle flexes its antennae; tints bleed in a beat after the engraved lines appear.

## Explainer structure
Three plates from a naturalist's folio, each pulled fresh under the press roller: engraved specimens of cats and dogs feed the circuit-lily "model", a life-cycle plate with fletched engraved arrows shows Guess, Check and Adjust, and a growth-stage plate counts accuracy in an engraved cartouche, with the colourist's red wash ringing the key moments.
