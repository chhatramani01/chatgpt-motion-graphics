# 29 · Linocut Relief Print

**Category:** Print

## Visual direction
A hand-carved two-colour relief print: tapering white gouge strokes cut from dense black, parallel carving for tone, a red reduction layer, salty uneven ink on cream rice paper, signed and numbered in pencil.

## Animation grammar
The proof is pulled twice — the red sheet peels back diagonally, then the black key — then the edition number, title and signature are pencilled in while a raking glint slides across the wet ink and the camera eases in.

## Explainer structure
Each beat is a newly carved block pulled as a fresh proof that peels back over the last one; arrows are gouged channels (the error path printed red), counters are carved numerals re-cut on twos, and emphasis is the printmaker's own pencil circle and margin note.
