# 83 · International Typographic Style

**Category:** Art & Design Movements

## Visual direction
A 1960s Zürich-school concert-style poster: strict 12-column modular grid, enormous flush-left grotesk set tight, an asymmetric field of rotating concentric arc segments over a red disc, and small precise info blocks hung from the grid — red, black and white only.

## Animation grammar
Rules draw out along the grid, the red disc opens and the ring segments spin in and decelerate until they lock onto a 22.5° module; the headline rises line by line out of its baseline masks and the info blocks step in column by column, then the rings drift like a slow mechanical score.

## Explainer structure
A Zürich-school information sequence on the same 12-column grid: the headline shrinks into a running header, pages change with a column-by-column red wipe, hairline rules with solid heads carry cause and flow, numbers count in Inter 800 and the key result gets a red block slammed behind it.
