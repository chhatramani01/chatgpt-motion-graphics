# 38 · Impasto Night

**Category:** Painterly

## Visual direction
Thick oil paint in short loaded strokes that follow a swirling flow field, Post-Impressionist night-sky style; every stroke stands off the canvas with a lit ridge and a shadowed edge, colour broken within each stroke.

## Animation grammar
The sky's strokes slowly rotate along their swirl and the halos around the moon and stars pulse ring by ring, as if the paint itself were turning.

## Explainer structure
The camera travels along one long swirling night-sky canvas; arrows, rings, labels and numbers are all laid on as fat lit daubs of paint (shadow edge, body, highlight ridge) in chrome yellow and vermilion against the blues.
