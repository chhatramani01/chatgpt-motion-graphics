# 34 · Cosmic Star Chart

**Category:** Cosmic & Psychedelic

## Visual direction
Deep-space illustration crossed with an engraved celestial atlas: fbm nebulae in teal, violet, rose and gold, a lit ringed gas giant with cratered moons, comets and a diffraction-spiked star, overlaid with hairline gold and pale-cyan chart work (dotted orbits with arrowheads, degree rings, ring markers, small-caps serif labels).

## Animation grammar
The sky fades up star by star while the camera drifts in, the key star flares, the planet lights up, then the chart engraves itself over it: degree ring sweeping round, orbits dotting on, markers and labels landing, a comet streaking past.

## Explainer structure
Told as one celestial chart overlaid on deep space: the model is a ringed planet whose moons are its weights, cat/dog examples arrive as constellation-headed comets, a warp streak changes scene, the guess is a chart beam to a labelled star that turns red with a red ring marker, the weights’ orbit visibly tilts, the camera tilts up to an atlas chart where the accuracy constellation forms and flares at 97%; numbers are big Syne figures over graduated sextant scales.
