# 74 · Bauhaus Poster

**Category:** Art & Design Movements

## Visual direction
A 1920s Bauhaus exhibition poster: primary red, yellow and blue plus black on aged cream stock, a robot face built only from circles, squares, semicircles and triangles on a strict grid, with constructed geometric lettering and lithographic ink mottle.

## Animation grammar
Built like a workshop paste-up: grid rules draw out, the halo and head drop in and snap with a little overshoot, features stamp on one by one, then the constructed letters are set block by block and the type follows; in the hold the pupils swivel and the antenna waves pulse.

## Explainer structure
Told as one long Bayer-style info-design sheet the camera tracks along and finally pulls back from, with thick bar-and-triangle arrows, tags that drop onto what they name, stamped Space Grotesk counters and a red ring drawn round the key moment.
