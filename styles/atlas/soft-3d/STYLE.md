# 56 · Soft 3D Clay

**Category:** Dimensional

## Visual direction
Dribbble-style “puffy” 3D icons: pastel matte-clay objects with chunky rounded extrusions, soft core shadows, bright bounce-light rims, glossy specular blobs and blurry contact shadows on a seamless pastel sweep.

## Animation grammar
Clay icons drop in one by one on springy squash-and-stretch with their contact shadows growing beneath them, the bulb flickers on, then the cursor flies in and taps the bubble while everything bobs at different depths.

## Explainer structure
The camera dollies across one wide pastel set with parallax depth-of-field (cards → clay chip with weight sliders → answer bubble → clay bar chart → pull back), arrows are puffy clay tubes with blobby heads, labels are squishy pills that spring onto objects, and numbers count inside butter-yellow clay badges.
