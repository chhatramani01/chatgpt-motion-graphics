# 67 · Memphis Group

**Category:** Art & Design Movements

## Visual direction
Early-80s Memphis Milano maximalism: pastel grounds clashing with bold primaries, thick black outlines and hard offset shadows, squiggles, zigzags, dot grids, terrazzo chips and confetti triangles, all orbiting a chunky tilted chat-bubble headline.

## Animation grammar
Cut-out cel timing on twos: the mint wedge slides in, shapes pop on one by one with springy overshoot, squiggles and zigzags draw themselves, the chat bubble squash-and-stretches in, the words pop on the beat, confetti bursts out and the sticker slaps on; then everything bobs off-beat and the typing dots bounce.

## Explainer structure
Memphis cut-out animation on twos: examples hop into a funnel-topped totem computer, scenes change with a striped polka-dot band that swipes across, arrows are fat outlined shapes with hard shadows, counters are stickers that pop on each tick, and the key moments get a spinning starburst.
