# 08 · 1940s Film Noir

**Category:** Light & Imaging

## Visual direction
A high-contrast black-and-white still from a forties detective picture: venetian-blind light slicing across a fedora silhouette, a glowing terminal as the only other light, cigarette smoke hanging in the beams, with heavy nitrate grain, gate weave, dust and scratches.

## Animation grammar
Blinds sway and smoke curls in a dark office; the terminal warms up with a CRT flash, its glow rim-lights the detective, a prompt types out, and the title card dissolves in word by word over projector flicker, weave and dirt.

## Explainer structure
Told as a 1940s newsreel / B-picture montage: spinning-newspaper headlines, iris, wipe and clock-wipe transitions, mugshot booking placards as labels, white newsreel arrows and lettering, a rolling mechanical odometer for numbers and a rubber stamp slammed down as the emphasis, all under heavy grain and gate weave.
