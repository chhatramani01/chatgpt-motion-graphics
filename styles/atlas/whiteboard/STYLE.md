# 04 · Whiteboard Explainer

**Category:** Hand-Drawn

## Visual direction
Dry-erase marker on a glossy whiteboard: streaky chisel-tip strokes with pooled edges, wobbly double-traced boxes and arrows, a ghost of the last erased lesson, and a soft ceiling-light sheen.

## Animation grammar
Each stroke draws on in sequence like a hand drawing it live, with labels written letter by letter and the sheen drifting slightly as the camera breathes.

## Explainer structure
A marker in hand draws the diagram while the camera follows it across one big board; the felt eraser wipes the loop away (leaving ghost residue) to make room for the accuracy chart, and numbers are re-scrawled in marker.
