# 36 · Thermal Camera

**Category:** Light & Imaging

## Visual direction
A handheld IR camera frame in ironbow: soft low-res heat field, cold glasses and warm breath, hot coffee and GPU racks, edge-enhanced outlines, sensor noise and the camera’s own HUD with spot meter, box measurement and scale bar.

## Animation grammar
Camera clicks through its shutter calibration, the auto-gain settles, coffee is hot-poured and steam rises, rack bands heat up bottom to top while the spot meter glides onto the mug and the readouts tick live.

## Explainer structure
Everything is filmed through the IR camera: scenes change with the shutter-calibration (NUC) click, heat is activity and a cold wave is the error signal, while the camera’s own HUD does the explaining with spot-meter arrows, measurement-box emphasis, tag labels riding on objects and live readouts.
