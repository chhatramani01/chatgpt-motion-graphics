# 42 · Wood Type Letterpress

**Category:** Print

## Visual direction
A two-ink wood-type broadside on cream stock: mixed display faces set block by block, each letter inked a little differently, wood grain and worn edges in the solids, ink squeezed to the rims and the whole forme bitten into the paper.

## Animation grammar
The forme is proofed block by block — rules first, then each wood letter lands with a heavy kiss of the platen, red and black alternating down the sheet — and the gears keep turning as the impression deepens.

## Explainer structure
Every beat is a new proof: the finished sheet is lifted off the press and a fresh one is laid down, then wood type, stock cuts of a cat and a dog, and arrows set from short rule pieces lock up block by block with a kiss of the platen; counters are re-set and re-stamped six times a second, and the wrong guess gets a red rubber-stamp WRONG.
