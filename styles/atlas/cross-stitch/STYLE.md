# 57 · Cross-Stitch Sampler

**Category:** Paper & Craft

## Visual direction
Pixel art worked as real counted cross-stitch on cream aida: every cell is an X of two-strand floss with sheen and twist, top legs all leaning the same way, dark backstitch outlines, all stretched in a wooden hoop.

## Animation grammar
The design fills in stitch by stitch in chart order, the needle hopping row to row trailing its floss, while the hoop drifts gently.

## Explainer structure
One long sampler slides through the hoop from motif to motif; everything is stitched cell by cell with the needle chasing the newest stitch, arrows and outlines are backstitch laid one hole at a time, numbers are re-stitched in a 3x5 sampler font, and the wrong guess gets a red running-stitch ring.
