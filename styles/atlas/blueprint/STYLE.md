# 37 · Engineering Blueprint

**Category:** Hand-Drawn

## Visual direction
A drafted cyanotype: white line-work bleeding slightly into mottled, sun-faded Prussian-blue paper, orthographic front/side views with a hatched section, dimension chains, a zoned border, title block and worn fold creases.

## Animation grammar
Construction and projection lines sweep across first, object lines ink in view by view, then dimensions and lettering pop in as the camera slowly pans along the sheet.

## Explainer structure
One large drafting sheet: a plotter head inks crisp white line-work and stencilled lettering while the camera tracks across and then down the sheet to the chart detail; the model is a gear-and-knob mechanism whose dials turn, arrows are drafted leaders, and numbers tick over in mono lettering inside dimension-style callouts.
