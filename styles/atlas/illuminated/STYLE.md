# 10 · Illuminated Manuscript

**Category:** Illustration

## Visual direction
A Book of Hours folio: mottled vellum with ruling and pricking, textura in iron-gall ink with vermilion rubrics, a historiated initial on raised burnished gold, lapis, rose and malachite with white-lead filigree, ivy-leaf rinceaux with gold bezants, and drolleries in the margins.

## Animation grammar
The scribe works the page: gold leaf is laid square by square and burnished to a travelling glint, pigments flood into the pen outlines, and the quill writes the text line by line.

## Explainer structure
Three folios of a manuscript, the leaf turning between them: champ initials bloom in gold, the examples are bestiary roundels flowing along a penwork arrow into a Brazen Head, the learning loop is a wheel of Guess, Check and Adjust where a marginal beast points out the error and the scribe corrects it in red, and accuracy rises as gilded bars until 97% turns to burnished gold.
