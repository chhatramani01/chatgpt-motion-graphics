# 61 · Kinetic Type

**Category:** Generative & Type

## Visual direction
A typographic music-video frame: condensed words cropped edge to edge with zero leading, filled vs outlined, strobe echoes, a slice glitch and one word stretched and filled with a halftone dreamscape — strictly two colours.

## Animation grammar
One word per beat at 120 bpm: THE punches in, MACHINES whips in from the right trailing outline echoes, LEARNED slams up with a one-frame inverted flash, the TO tab wipes up, DREAM springs open and its halftone moon develops; then the slice glitch and echoes pulse on every beat.

## Explainer structure
Words slam in on the beat and do the explaining; scenes change with a slice-glitch tear and a whip-pan, arrows are heavy rules with chevrons, labels are inverted mono tags that drop onto their targets, and the counters are huge Anton numerals with a strobe-flash on the key hits.
