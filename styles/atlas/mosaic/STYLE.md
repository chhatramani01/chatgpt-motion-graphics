# 62 · Byzantine Mosaic

**Category:** Illustration

## Visual direction
A Byzantine wall mosaic built from thousands of hand-cut tesserae laid in rows that echo every contour (andamento), with mortar grout, bevelled tile edges, per-tile colour variation and a glinting gold-smalti ground.

## Animation grammar
Light rakes across the wall so individual gold smalti flash in turn; tiles could be laid row by row outward from the figure.

## Explainer structure
Every scene is a fresh andamento-tessellated wall: tiles are laid element by element (arrows tile by tile along their length), scene changes pry the old tesserae up and re-lay the wall in a sweeping front, counters are live tesserae re-set on a grid, and gold smalti flash on the key moments.
