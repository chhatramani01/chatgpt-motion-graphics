# 18 · Claymation Set

**Category:** Paper & Craft

## Visual direction
A plasticine stop-motion still: chunky hand-squished characters and props with soft waxy subsurface shading, thumbprints, tool marks and lumpy silhouettes on a sculpted clay set, shot with shallow depth of field.

## Animation grammar
Animated on twos: the character boils subtly between frames, fingerprints shift, the waving arm snaps pose-to-pose and the chat-bubble dots pop like little clay balls.

## Explainer structure
Animated on twos on one clay set: every piece is a baked plasticine lump that the "animator" pops on with squash-and-stretch, slides along the table or knocks off so it falls out of frame; arrows are rolled clay coils, labels and the counter are pressed clay letters re-sculpted each time the number changes, and the wrong guess gets a red clay X slapped on with a shake.
