# 48 · Transit Map

**Category:** Data & Information

## Visual direction
A Beck-style metro diagram of an AI pipeline: fat coloured lines locked to 0/45/90 degrees with rounded bends, line-colour station ticks, black-ringed interchanges, a river and a boxed legend, all in clean sans lettering.

## Animation grammar
Title and key settle, then each line draws out from its terminus in turn with stations ticking on and interchanges popping as the line passes; once the network is complete, small trains glide along every route.

## Explainer structure
The lesson is a journey on the network: the camera rides along a Data Line into the Model interchange, round a Training Loop line (Guess, Check, Adjust stations) and out along an Accuracy Line, while an amber dot-matrix departure board counts examples and accuracy and finally flips to the takeaway.
