# 16 · Oscilloscope Vector Art

**Category:** Retro Digital

## Visual direction
Oscilloscope-music XY art on an analogue lab scope: one P31 green beam draws everything, bright where it slows at turns and dim on fast retrace lines, with bloom and halation behind an internal 8x10 graticule, framed by a moulded CRT bezel and a knobbed instrument front panel.

## Animation grammar
Power-on: the dot blooms, the timebase pulls it into a sine sweep, the X-Y button snaps in and the waveform folds into a circle that winds itself into a spinning spiral-sphere while the beam writes the vector text.

## Explainer structure
Everything is drawn by one scope beam: transitions are the instrument's own (picture collapses to a dot and re-expands, X gain to zero then the timebase sweep, Y gain to zero back to X-Y), arrows and Hershey-style labels are beam strokes, counters are redrawn vector numerals, and the wrong guess shudders amber while the 97% flares the bloom.
