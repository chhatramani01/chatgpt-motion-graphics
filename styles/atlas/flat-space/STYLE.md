# 01 · Flat-Vector Science

**Category:** Popular Explainer Looks

## Visual direction
Flat-vector science-explainer illustration: deep navy-to-violet space gradients, chunky outline-free shapes with rich multi-stop gradients, flat crescent shadows and rim highlights, soft radial glows, sparkly star fields, banded ringed planets and cute big-eyed round characters.

## Animation grammar
Smooth eased motion with gentle overshoot: the camera pulls back through scales, planets drift with parallax, characters bob and pop in, glows pulse.

## Explainer structure
Big smooth camera moves through scale: the model pulls back until a million examples become a spiral galaxy, a foreground planet sweeps across as the wipe, and the camera dives into the glowing brain dome to emerge at a planet whose ring is the accuracy bar; arrows are chunky dotted rounded curves, numbers are big rounded Nunito.
