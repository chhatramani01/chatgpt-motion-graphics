# 82 · ASCII Demoscene

**Category:** Retro Digital

## Visual direction
A ray-marched 3D torus rasterised into a strict monospace character grid via a luminance ramp, colour-coded like a 90s demoscene intro: box-drawn title panel, plasma backdrop, copper bars and a sine scroller.

## Animation grammar
Like a demo intro booting: plasma thickens in, the spinning torus is rastered into characters top-to-bottom behind a bright scan row, the block title and nfo panel print, copper bars open and a rainbow sine-scroller glides in from the right.

## Explainer structure
One long ASCII document that the terminal scrolls through row by row: figlet block-letter question, labelled ASCII cat/dog cards stepping along a box-drawing arrow into a MODEL box, a reverse-video GUESS/CHECK/ADJUST loop with a red error path, and block-character bar charts with figlet counters.
