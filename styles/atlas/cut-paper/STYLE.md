# 24 · Cut-Paper Diorama

**Category:** Paper & Craft

## Visual direction
A layered cardstock shadow-box: every shape is a separate scissor-cut sheet with fibre texture, a lit paper edge and a soft drop shadow onto the sheet behind it; torn edges show the white core of the paper.

## Animation grammar
Layers slide in on parallax like a pop-up book, the rocket rises on its wire while paper smoke puffs and the flame tongues flutter.

## Explainer structure
One long paper diorama the camera pans along on parallax; arrows are cut-paper strips that grow, labels are paper tags dropped onto what they name, numbers are swapped cut-out digit tiles, and emphasis is a paper ring or sunburst popping up behind.
