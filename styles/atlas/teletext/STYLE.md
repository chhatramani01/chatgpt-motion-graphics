# 79 · Teletext Page

**Category:** Retro UI & Neon

## Visual direction
A broadcast teletext page: strict 40x25 character grid, eight saturated colours, 2x3 sextant mosaic graphics with one colour per cell, double-height headlines and Fastext links, seen through a CRT.

## Animation grammar
Only the header shows while the page counter rolls in green, then the page lands top-down two rows at a time; mosaic bars step up a sub-block at a time, LATEST and the robot eyes flash, the ticker hops whole characters and the clock ticks.

## Explainer structure
Each beat is a new teletext page (P100-P104): the header searches in green while the old page lingers, then the new page overwrites it two rows at a time; arrows, pictures and bar charts are sextant mosaics, numbers count in double-height digits and the key moments use the Flash attribute.
