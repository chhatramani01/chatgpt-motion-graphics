# 66 · Hidden-Line Wireframe

**Category:** Dimensional

## Visual direction
Vector-display 3D: a perspective wire-mesh landscape with true hidden-line removal (back-to-front quads filled with the night before stroking), a wireframe moon, phosphor-cyan lines with bloom on deep navy, and HUD coordinate labels.

## Animation grammar
HUD brackets snap on, the wireframe moon traces itself, then the hidden-line mesh is scanned in row by row from the camera to the horizon behind a bright scan front; markers lock on and labels type out while the camera glides forward and the scan keeps sweeping.

## Explainer structure
Learning is shown as descent over the hidden-line “loss landscape”: the camera flies the mesh from a data-feed HUD streaming into the wireframe-sphere model, down to an amber “model weights” marker whose error becomes a dashed downhill vector, then follows it into the glowing basin; labels are bracketed HUD tags that type on, numbers are big Space Mono readouts.
