# 80 · Silkscreen Grid

**Category:** Print

## Visual direction
A Warhol-style repeat portrait: the same robot pulled eight times through a grainy black photo screen over loose, hand-cut flat colour plates in garish combinations, each pull misregistered and streaked by the squeegee.

## Animation grammar
Panels are printed one after another across the grid — background flood, then the loose colour shapes slap down, then the squeegee drags the black photo screen across with a wet ink bead — before a slow push-in on the finished wall.

## Explainer structure
Each beat is its own silkscreened panel, pulled in order (flood, loose colour shapes, squeegee drags the black screen) while the camera travels the grid; black arrows are painted across the gutters between panels, counters are stencil type, emphasis is a colourway flicker, and the final pull-back reveals all six beats as one Warhol grid.
