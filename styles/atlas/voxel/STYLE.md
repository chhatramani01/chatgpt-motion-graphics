# 31 · Voxel Diorama

**Category:** Dimensional

## Visual direction
MagicaVoxel-style diorama: a floating voxel island with a waving robot, chunky trees and a pond — three-tone cube faces, per-vertex ambient occlusion in the creases, ray-marched soft shadows and a warm toy palette.

## Animation grammar
Voxels rain down and stack layer by layer from the island’s rocky underside up to the robot’s antenna while the camera orbits; the robot’s eyes flicker on, it starts waving, and the pixel title drops in block by block.

## Explainer structure
One long voxel island the camera trucks along while each zone is rebuilt from falling voxels; arrows are chains of little cubes, cards and the ✗/✓ verdict are real voxel pixel-art, the robot’s chest equaliser re-levels as its weights adjust, and numbers tick in Silkscreen on extruded block tags.
