# 02 · Watercolor Wash

**Category:** Painterly

## Visual direction
Transparent pigment glazed in dozens of ragged low-opacity layers on cold-press paper: dark pooled edges, wet-in-wet bleeds, cauliflower blooms, granulation and untouched white paper for the lights, over a faint pencil sketch.

## Animation grammar
Each wash floods in from its pencil outline, edges darken as the water dries and blooms creep outward; the title is brushed in stroke by stroke.

## Explainer structure
One long sketchbook sheet the camera pans along; arrows are loaded round-brush strokes with pooled edges, labels and the counter are brush-lettered and re-written as they change, and red alizarin circles do the emphasis.
