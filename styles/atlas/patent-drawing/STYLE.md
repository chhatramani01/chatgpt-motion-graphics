# 73 · Patent Drawing

**Category:** Illustration

## Visual direction
A 1930s US patent sheet: ruling-pen black ink line art with shade lines, parallel-line and stipple shading, section hatching, reference numerals on curved leaders, typeset header and inked signatures on yellowed paper.

## Animation grammar
Lines are inked one stroke at a time like a draughtsman at work — outlines first, then hatching, then numerals — while the FIG. 3 gear train slowly turns.

## Explainer structure
The camera travels along one long patent sheet as FIG. 4–6 are inked stroke by stroke: ruled arrows and leader lines tie labels to parts, odometer drums count the examples and the accuracy, and an examiner's red pencil rings the wrong guess and the 97%.
