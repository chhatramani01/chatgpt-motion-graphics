# 63 · Y2K Liquid Chrome

**Category:** Retro UI & Neon

## Visual direction
Millennium-futurist liquid chrome: environment-mapped metaball blobs and inflated chrome bubble letters with thin-film iridescence, soap bubbles, starburst glints and frosted pill UI in silver, ice-blue and lilac.

## Animation grammar
Mercury droplets fly in, overshoot and merge into one blob, chrome letters inflate one by one with a springy pop, frosted pills glide up, and reflections, bubbles and glints keep sliding and twinkling.

## Explainer structure
A glossy millennium interface explains it: the model is a living mercury blob that liquid-morphs to a new spot at every scene change while frosted-glass panels swoosh in and out, arrows are iridescent ribbons, labels are glossy pills, and the counter and 97% are inflated chrome bubble digits.
