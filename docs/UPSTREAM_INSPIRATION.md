# Upstream inspiration and ownership

We studied [Priyanshsharma21/aansh-video](https://github.com/Priyanshsharma21/aansh-video), especially its core workflow, podcast/personal explainer recipes and word timing/mouth processing concepts. Our 46 format-specific agents are original rewrites for ChatGPT; they are **not** copies of upstream skill files. We credit the original creator for these ideas. Do not infer that this project is affiliated with them.

Our 86 visual prompts reproduce the user-provided textual fingerprints from `data.js`, based on [Futurepedia's AI Style Atlas](https://www.futurepedia.io/resources/ai-style-atlas/view). We provide external links to posters and videos as study references. They are not licensed assets bundled in this repo. New scripts and workflow instructions are independently authored.
